Health Benefits

Apples are highly nutritious, containing antioxidants, vitamins, dietary fiber, and a range of other nutrients. Due to their varied nutrient content, they may help prevent several health conditions including cancer, obesity, heart disease, and diabetes.

How to Store

Store apples in the crisper drawer of your refrigerator. They prefer a cool, humid environment. Keep them away from strong-smelling foods like onions or garlic, as apples can absorb odors. If kept at room temperature, they will ripen faster.
